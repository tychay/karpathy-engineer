# Workflow: Goal Setting

<required_reading>
None — this workflow depends on the `grill-me` skill, not on create-agent-skills reference files.
</required_reading>

<process>
Always invoke `grill-me` to get to the success criteria — do not pre-judge whether the goal or core decisions are known or final yourself.

grill-me phase 1 (summarize in 3-6 bullets, "yes grill that" or "adjust") is the cheap
rubber-stamp path when the goal really is settled; let it decide, not you.
</process>

<success_criteria>
This workflow is complete when:
- [ ] The **real** goal is explicit and agreed, not assumed.
- [ ] Any core decision the project intends to drive is named.
- [ ] Ready to move to `project-planning.md`.
</success_criteria>
